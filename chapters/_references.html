Corrections and independent developments

Caption text is evidence of the recording's topic and timing, not an error-free mathematical authority. Publisher labels do not prove human transcription. Chapters distinguish direct source discussion from completed proofs and original examples. The per-chapter .sources.json files list source intervals, inspected references, teaching additions, scope decisions, and limits; the combined analysis ledger binds them to the final manuscript hashes.

Examples of substantive clarifications include differentiation in positive characteristic, the direction of coordinate changes, the failure of a general kernel–range direct sum, diagonal repetition versus minimal exponents, and the column-coordinate orientation of Jordan chains. Chapter25 replaces one caption-ambiguous matrix with an explicitly original verified example. These repairs are not attributed to the lecturer as exact statements.
