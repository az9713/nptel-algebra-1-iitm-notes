Algebra 1: Foundations

From precise statements and quotient constructions to the structure of a linear operator. An independent textbook edition of Jaikrishnan J's NPTEL course at IIT Palakkad, with proofs, concrete examples, executable checks, and fully worked exercises.

The 46-video source playlist is shuffled in blocks. This edition follows the official course's 45-lesson teaching order, with the introduction first. IIT Madras is the coordinating institution.

Read in sequence, choose a route in the study guide, or inspect the recording map. All mathematical rendering and reading controls work offline. Videos and external references require an internet connection.
